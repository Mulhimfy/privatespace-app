Privacy policy · effective 8 November 2026

Your space stays yours.

This policy covers the Android app Private Space (package com.privatespace.app) published by Mulhimfy.

In short

  • Private Space collects no personal data and shares nothing with anyone.
  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything off your phone.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app does on your phone

Private Space creates an Android work profile on your phone (Android's built-in way to keep a separate set of apps) and becomes its administrator ("profile owner"). It uses that role only to do what you ask in the app:

  • copy apps that are already on your phone into the space, or open the Play Store there;
  • hide and show apps in the space, open them, and remove them;
  • allow or block sharing files between your personal apps and the space, when you change that setting;
  • delete the space when you ask, which removes every app and file inside it.

The app reads the list of apps installed on your phone and their names and icons, so you can choose what to add. This list is used on the phone only and is never stored elsewhere or sent anywhere.

Apps you put in the space are separate copies. Their own data and their own privacy policies are handled by those apps, not by Private Space. Private Space does not read, copy or monitor the content of any app.

App lock

If you set a PIN, only a salted, one-way hash of it is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Fingerprint or face unlock uses Android's own system prompt: Private Space never receives your fingerprint or face data, only a yes or no from Android.

Storage, backup and deletion

Settings (such as your lock options) are stored only in the app's private storage. They are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Deleting the space in the app's settings deletes the work profile and everything in it.

Children

Private Space is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
